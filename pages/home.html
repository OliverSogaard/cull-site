What it does, and does not do

A culling tool, not another editor.

01

Verdicts land where Lightroom reads them

Keep is a pick flag, reject is a reject flag, favorite adds a purple label. Written as an XMP sidecar, or into the metadata of a DNG. Import the folder and it is already sorted.

02

The image in your RAW file is never changed

CULL reads the preview your camera already embedded. It never decodes or rewrites sensor data, and nothing moves or copies until you say so.

03

Fast because it reads less

A folder of three thousand frames opens in seconds. Bursts are grouped by the camera's own drive mode, and a smart pass suggests the losers of a burst before you get there.
