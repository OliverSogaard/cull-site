How a license works

  • The key arrives by email after payment. In CULL, open Settings → License, paste it and press Add license.
  • A key works on two computers at a time. To move it, remove it on one computer (Settings → License) and enter it on the other.
  • CULL confirms the license online about every twelve hours and keeps working offline for 30 days.
  • Lost the key? Write to support from the email address you bought with.
  • Changed your mind? Write within 14 days of the purchase and the price is refunded in full.
