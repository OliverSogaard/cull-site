Formats

Eight RAW formats, measured body by body.

CULL never decodes sensor data. Every view is the JPEG your camera already embedded in the file, read by exact byte range, which is why a folder of thousands opens in seconds. What each format gives depends on what its bodies embed, and the app says so instead of pretending: where the largest embedded JPEG is smaller than the sensor, a quiet "preview · N px" badge appears while you zoom.

Where verdicts go

For every format but DNG, verdicts are written to an XMP sidecar next to the file: keep is a pick flag, reject is a reject flag, favorite is a pick with a purple label. Lightroom Classic reads them on import. A DNG carries its own metadata block, and Lightroom ignores a sidecar next to one, so for a DNG the verdict is written into that block. In both cases the image data is never touched.

Not supported

Pentax PEF, Samsung SRW, Hasselblad 3FR, Sigma X3F and the old Canon CRW. Most of those cameras can also write DNG, which CULL opens.
